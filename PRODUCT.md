# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners, building/condominium administrators, and industrial/commercial decision-makers in Colombia evaluating photovoltaic solar energy systems. Also includes health/pharmaceutical sector operators (clinics, labs, pharmaceutical plants) as a secondary B2B audience with stricter power-continuity needs.

## Product Purpose

INTELION designs, installs, and maintains photovoltaic solar energy systems for homes, buildings, and industry, turning solar resource into measurable energy savings, operational efficiency, and long-term sustainability.

## Positioning

A single technical ally across the entire solar project lifecycle — consumption analysis, engineering/sizing, certified installation, and ongoing monitoring/support — using certified, world-class component brands, and fluent in Colombia's Ley 1715 tax incentives (renta deduction, IVA exemption, accelerated depreciation) that a generic installer would not surface.

## Operating Context

Colombian market. Offers on-grid, hybrid, and off-grid (aislado) systems, battery storage/backup integration, and remote monitoring/control. Serves residential autoconsumo, common-area systems for buildings/condominiums (elevators, water pumps), large-scale industrial/commercial installations, and mission-critical health/pharmaceutical facilities requiring uninterrupted, clean-quality power for electromedical equipment and cold chains.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Full lifecycle services: análisis y diagnóstico → diseño e ingeniería → instalación certificada → monitoreo y soporte.
- Component categories offered: paneles solares, inversores, baterías, protecciones, estructura, cableado, conectores.
- Backed brands (from brochure, page 8): Risen, ZNShine Solar, Estar Solar, Canadian Solar, Felicity Solar, Dyness, Artsign, Leader, Must, EPEVER, Sineng, Procet Scientific, Victron Energy, Pylontech, Legrand, Kibor Cable, Procables/Prysmian.
- Colombia-specific regulatory hook: Ley 1715 — 50% renta deduction (up to 15 years), 0% IVA on core components, up to 33.3%/year accelerated depreciation. Typical industrial ROI cited: 3–5 years.

## Product Principles

1. Show the full-lifecycle ally positioning (analysis → design → install → monitor), not just "we sell panels."
2. Speak to all three core segments (hogar, edificio, industria) without over-indexing on one, per user direction — but keep messaging general/institutional in tone.
3. Let certified technology and brand backing (real logos/components) carry credibility instead of invented social proof.
4. Keep the logo/isotype (leaf + sun + circuit) as the recurring visual anchor across hero and panels; real photography supports it (2026-08-06) rather than replacing it.
